Dr Sam's Academy — Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026 · Applies to the web app, the iOS app, and the Android app

The short version

Dr Sam's Academy collects nothing. No accounts, no names sent anywhere, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind. Everything a child does in the academy stays on the device it happened on.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves practice progress so a child can pick up where they left off: profile names typed on the device, the grade picked for each profile, stars, coins, streaks, and per-question history used by the no-repeat rule. This is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never transmitted, and we could not read it if we wanted to, because nothing in the app sends data off the device.

What the app collects about your child

Nothing. The app makes no network requests after it loads. It works fully offline. There are no third-party services, no software development kits from advertisers or analytics companies, and no server of ours receiving anything.

Children's privacy

The academy is built for children in kindergarten through fifth grade, and it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the most direct way possible: by collecting no personal information at all.

Removing data

Deleting a profile inside the app (Grown-ups tab, press and hold) or uninstalling the app removes everything, because everything lives on the device.
